Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Scani ("we", "our", "the app") is a menu translation app that helps users understand Japanese restaurant menus. Your privacy matters to us.

What data we collect

Scani does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location, and does not require account creation, login, or registration. We do collect anonymous usage data to understand how the app is used and improve it (see Analytics & Usage Data below).

Camera and Photos

The app accesses your camera and photo library solely to scan menu images for translation. Photos are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. If you submit a report, menu images and selected issues are sent to our team via a secure webhook to help improve translation quality. Photos are not stored on our servers beyond what is needed to process your report.

When a scan returns no results or fails (no menu items detected, response truncated, etc.), the menu image you took may be temporarily uploaded to our private storage for diagnostic purposes — so we can review what went wrong and improve the translation pipeline. These images are anonymous (no identifier embedded in the file), kept only as long as needed to investigate, and then permanently deleted. They are never shared with third parties.

On-Device Processing

Text recognition (Apple Vision) and speech pronunciation (AVSpeechSynthesizer) run entirely on your device. No data is shared with third parties for these features.

Analytics & Usage Data

Scani collects anonymous usage data — such as the number of scans, app launches, onboarding completion, camera permission grants, which features are used, app version, and language — to understand how the app is used and improve it. This data is tied to a random, anonymous identifier generated on your device; it is not linked to your name, email, or any account (Scani has none) and cannot be used to identify you. This data is stored on our Supabase backend.

Menu Items Corpus

To improve translation quality over time and reduce reliance on third-party AI for items we've already seen, Scani uploads the Japanese-language text of menu items extracted from your scans to our private backend. Only the Japanese title text is sent — no images, no translations, no prices, and no identifier of any kind, so the data cannot be linked back to you or your device. These titles describe public restaurant menu items and contain no personal information.

Advertising

Scani uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Per-impression ad revenue values returned by AdMob are also logged alongside the anonymous identifier so we can attribute revenue to scan sessions. This data is handled by Google according to their privacy policy.

On-Device Data

Scan history and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API (menu translation)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Apps Script (report processing)
  • Supabase (anonymous usage analytics storage, temporary storage of failed-scan images for diagnostics, menu items corpus storage)

Children's Privacy

Scani is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
